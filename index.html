Professional Introduction

I'm an ACCA member and SOCPA Associate based in Al Khobar, Saudi Arabia, with six years of experience in financial accounting, cost control, compliance, and audit support across multi-entity, multi-currency operations in Saudi Arabia's food & beverage sector. My work sits at the intersection of technical accuracy — IFRS reporting, Saudi VAT and ZATCA compliance, internal controls — and practical business impact, from working-capital and receivables improvement to audit readiness. Coordinating across finance, operations, procurement, HR, banks, and auditors has been part of that work throughout. I'm currently building toward FP&A, strategic finance, and finance business partnering, with CIA certification in progress.
