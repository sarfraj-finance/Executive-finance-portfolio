Executive Profile

Finance professional combining technical control with commercial progression

I build financial control that holds up under scrutiny, and I'm now extending that discipline into analysis that shapes decisions before they're made. Six years across multi-entity Saudi operations have given me depth in financial reporting, IFRS-compliant statements, and the regulatory detail of Saudi VAT and ZATCA e-invoicing — compliance work that either holds or doesn't, with no partial credit. Internal controls and audit readiness have been a constant thread: segregation of duties, three-way matching, and documentation that stands up when an external auditor tests it. Working capital has been where that technical foundation met commercial impact, most directly in a sustained reduction in receivables ageing. None of this happened in isolation — it required coordinating consistently across finance, operations, procurement, HR, banks, and auditors. That cross-functional habit is now the bridge into FP&A and finance business partnering, where the same rigor applies to numbers that haven't happened yet.

“Finance should not only explain what happened. It should improve what happens next.”
